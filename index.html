Our Story

About S K OHENE ENTERPRISE

S K OHENE ENTERPRISE was created with a simple idea: dressing well shouldn't come with an unreasonable price tag. We believe every man deserves to feel confident and put-together, without having to overspend to get there.

From our shop at Kwabenya University Junction in Accra, we curate men's clothing, caps and accessories that balance quality, style and everyday affordability. Every piece is chosen with care, and every order is handled with the kind of attention we'd want as customers ourselves — good service, honest pricing and delivery you can count on.

Luxury, to us, isn't about the price on the tag. It's about how you feel wearing it. That's what we mean when we say: Luxury Within Reach.

How do I place an order?

Browse the shop, add the items you want to your cart along with your chosen size and color, then go to checkout. You'll enter your details and send your order directly to us on WhatsApp.

How does WhatsApp ordering work?

Once you complete your order details on the checkout page, clicking "Send Order via WhatsApp" opens WhatsApp with a message already containing your full order — items, sizes, colors, quantities and total. You just review and send it to us.
